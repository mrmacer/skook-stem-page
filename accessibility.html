Accessibility

Our commitment to making STEM@Skook29 useful to people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and ways of navigating.

Our Commitment

Working toward WCAG 2.2 AA

STEM@Skook29 is committed to providing an accessible website and uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as its target. Accessibility is an ongoing practice, not a one-time claim, and the site will continue to be reviewed and improved as content and features change.

Built-in support

The site includes skip links, semantic page landmarks, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, responsive navigation, and keyboard-operable interactive components.

Ongoing improvement

New pages, documents, and interactive features will be reviewed with accessibility in mind. Feedback from people using assistive technology is especially valuable.

Report a Barrier

Tell us what is not working

If you cannot access content or operate a feature, use the contact form or email maceg@iu29.org. If possible, include the page address, a brief description of the barrier, the device or browser you used, and the format or accommodation that would help. Do not include student information.

Known Limitation

Linked documents and services

Some linked PDFs and third-party registration or resource sites are outside STEM@Skook29's direct control and may not provide the same accessibility experience. If one of these resources creates a barrier, contact us and we will help identify an alternative when one is available.
