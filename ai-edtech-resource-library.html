How We Curate

Useful, Honest, School-Appropriate

The library favors authoritative guidance, classroom usefulness, transparent limitations, and resources that preserve educator and student agency. Inclusion is not district approval.

  • Generic affiliate listicles
  • Consumer companion chatbots
  • AI detectors presented as reliable proof
  • Deepfake generators without clear instructional value
  • Products with unclear ownership or privacy policies
  • Tools designed to bypass school systems
  • Abandoned products

About Verification

Live verified means the page and reviewed facts were checked on the listed date.

Curated — live verification pending means an established resource is included from catalog review and queued for its first live check.

Confidence labels describe the strength and completeness of the reviewed information, not district approval.
