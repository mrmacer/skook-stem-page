Why Partner with Us

The Region Succeeds
When We Build Together

Schuylkill County's economic future depends on a pipeline of skilled, curious, STEM-ready students. That pipeline is built in classrooms, makerspaces, and competitions — and it's built better with you.

Partnering with STEM@Skook29 gives your organization direct access to the region's future workforce — while making a real, visible difference in education for the kids in your community.

🏭 Manufacturing 💻 Technology 🏥 Healthcare ⚡ Energy 🌱 Environment 🏗️ Engineering

🎯

Direct Workforce Pipeline

Connect with students before they graduate. Build relationships early and hire from a pool that already knows your industry.

📣

Brand Visibility

Your brand in front of students, parents, teachers, and administrators across 12 public school districts.

💚

Community Impact

Genuine corporate citizenship — not a logo on a flyer, but a real, documented impact on education in your region.
