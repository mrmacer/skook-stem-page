What is this?

A network for the people shaping STEM in our region.

We are building connections between the people teaching STEM, the people doing STEM, and the organizations creating opportunities around STEM.

01 / CONNECT

Meet the ecosystem.

Meet educators, employers, organizations, and community leaders working across Schuylkill County.

02 / COLLABORATE

Find the overlap.

Identify needs, resources, expertise, and opportunities that make sense to bring together.

03 / BUILD

Make something useful.

Turn those connections into student experiences, partnerships, projects, programs, and opportunities.

No single level of commitment

What participation can look like

You do not have to sign up for another standing committee. Start with what fits your organization right now.

  • Attend an ecosystem gathering
  • Share expertise or industry insight
  • Host or support a student experience
  • Connect educators with people doing the work
  • Collaborate on a project or challenge
  • Share equipment, facilities, resources, or opportunities
  • Help identify workforce needs
  • Sponsor or support an initiative
  • Come listen and see where your organization fits
