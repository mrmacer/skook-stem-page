For In-Building Advisors

CSO Advisor Center

A practical year-at-a-glance guide for the teacher who helps Chief Science Officers navigate plans, reporting, events, permissions, and school coordination.

2×at least each month

Keep a Reliable Meeting Rhythm

The CSO Advisor meets at least twice a month with their CSO or CSOs. These check-ins support the ongoing work described throughout this guide.

Advisor Responsibilities

What the In-Building Advisor Supports

The role connects student leadership work with the people, processes, and permissions inside the school or district.

Action Plan Implementation

Help CSOs implement their Action Plans and maintain review checkpoints through the year.

Online Activity

Review LMS or Google Classroom activity connected to the program.

Mentor and School Connections

Support interactions with mentors, the school, and the district.

Permissions and Attendance

Handle permission slips, transportation requests, and excused absences.

Administrative Coordination

Coordinate with school administration as CSOs carry out program responsibilities.

Cabinet Meetings

Attend Cabinet Meetings alongside the school's CSO or CSOs.

Year at a Glance

Advisor Checklist

Use this sequence to keep recurring meetings, reports, events, permissions, and reflection visible throughout the program year.

  1. Ongoing: Meet Twice Monthly

    Meet with CSOs at least twice each month and review LMS or Google Classroom activity.

  2. LTI: Quarter 1 Report

    Support required LTI participation and keep the Quarter 1 reporting checkpoint visible.

  3. Fall Cabinet: Quarter 2 Report

    Attend alongside CSOs and support permissions, transportation requests, and excused absences.

  4. Spring Cabinet: Quarter 3 Report

    Attend alongside CSOs and continue Action Plan review and implementation support.

  5. April 1: Reflection Due

    Keep the year-end Reflection deadline visible as CSOs document their implementation.

  6. End of Year: Quarter 4 Report

    Support the final Quarterly Report checkpoint at the End of Year Celebration.

Practical Toolbox

Processes to Keep Ready

Use your school or district's current procedures, and contact IU29 for current CSO documents rather than relying on an unverified download.

School Process

Permission, Absence, and Transportation

Coordinate permission slips, excused absences, transportation requests, and administration before each event.

Program Checkpoint

Action Plan Review

Meet with CSOs and the school team after LTI, then support administrator approval before the November 1 submission deadline.

Program Checkpoint

Reports and Reflection

Track Q1 at LTI, Q2 at Fall Cabinet, Q3 at Spring Cabinet, Q4 at the End of Year Celebration, and the Reflection due April 1.

School Process

Media Consent

Track parent or guardian media consent before any student photos are used publicly.

Need current forms? Contact IU29 for the documents and process guidance currently in use.
