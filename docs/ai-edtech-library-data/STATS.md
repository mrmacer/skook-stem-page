## Excluded resources and reasons

- **Consumer ChatGPT / Claude.ai / Gemini consumer accounts as student tools** — Consumer terms (13+ with parental consent, or 18+) and consumer data handling are not a K-12 deployment path; the education-specific products above are the correct records.
- **Perplexity** — Capable research assistant, but no verified K-12-specific terms, student account model, or education data protections at review time; revisit if an education offering ships.
- **AI writing detectors as standalone records (GPTZero, Turnitin AI detection, etc.)** — Per exclusion rules, detectors are not presented as reliable proof of misconduct; the detector-bias research record (int-detector-research) carries the caution instead.
- **Character.AI and companion-style chatbots** — Entertainment companion design, documented safety incidents, and litigation history; no instructional rationale that survives the risk profile.
- **ISTE/GM AI Explorations hands-on guides** — Historically good, but aging content in a fast-moving field; superseded in this library by Experience AI, Day of AI, and Code.org.
- **EdTech Evidence Exchange** — Organization sunset; links unreliable. ASSISTments and RAND records carry the evidence torch.
- **Generic 'Top 100 AI Tools for Teachers' listicles** — Affiliate-driven, unvetted, and privacy-blind by construction; categorically excluded.
- **Snapchat My AI and similar in-app consumer assistants** — Consumer social product with no school controls; relevant to family digital-citizenship conversations only.
- **Deepfake/faceswap generators** — No instructional rationale sufficient to offset misuse risk in a public K-12 library; synthetic-media literacy is covered via CAI and media literacy records.
