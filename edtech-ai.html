STEELS Lesson Hook Generator

Try a lightweight AI-style planning tool built to spark STEELS-aligned lesson ideas. Select a grade band, topic, and activity style — and see what a well-structured lesson hook looks like.

1
Choose your context Pick a grade band, STEELS topic, and activity style that fits your class right now.
2
Generate a hook Get an opening question, hands-on activity idea, STEELS connection, and a suggested student product — instantly.
3
Adapt and use it Copy the idea, remix it for your classroom, or use it as a starting point for your own planning.
🤖

This tool demonstrates how structured AI-style planning logic can support instructional design — without requiring a live AI connection. Every output is grounded in STEELS standards and real classroom practice. No login required. No data collected.

How could this work with a real AI?

This demo uses curated, expert-written content paired with selection logic — no live AI call. A production version could use a real language model to generate infinite, personalized variations. IU29 is exploring what that could look like for Schuylkill County teachers.
