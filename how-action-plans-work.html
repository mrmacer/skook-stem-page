Chief Science Officers

How Action Plans Work

A Chief Science Officer moves from an idea at LTI to a school-approved proposal, documented implementation, and year-end reflection.

Plan Development

From Brainstorm to Approved Proposal

The process begins at the Leadership Training Institute and returns to the student's school team before the final proposal is shared.

Brainstorm at LTI

CSOs generate ideas and complete an Initial Action Plan Form during the required Leadership Training Institute.

Meet With the School Team

Within one month after LTI, CSOs meet with their school team and CSO Advisor to develop a Final Action Plan proposal.

Secure Administrator Approval

The school site administrator reviews and approves the Final Action Plan.

Share the Final Plan

The approved proposal is shared with CSO International through the online platform by November 1.

Students may work together or independently: A CSO may complete a group Action Plan or an individual Action Plan.
Progress Reporting

Four Reports Across the Year

Quarterly Reports document what CSOs have done and ask them to estimate how many people their work has impacted.

Quarter 1

At LTI

Quarter 1 is submitted at the Leadership Training Institute.

Quarter 2

Fall Cabinet Meeting

Quarter 2 is submitted at the Fall Cabinet Meeting.

Quarter 3

Spring Cabinet Meeting

Quarter 3 is submitted at the Spring Cabinet Meeting.

Quarter 4

End of Year Celebration

Quarter 4 is submitted at the End of Year Celebration.

Reflection due April 1

Implementation Ends With Reflection

After completing the Action Plan, each CSO writes a Reflection on how the plan was implemented. The Reflection is due April 1.

Skill Progression

Leadership Develops Across Four Levels

The progression tracks growth across STEM and workforce exposure, CSO community participation, project scope, and ambassador reach.

  • STEM/workforce exposure
  • CSO community participation
  • Project scope
  • Ambassador reach

School

Level 2

Community

Level 3

Leadership

Level 4
