Building the future of learning in Schuylkill County through creativity, computational thinking, and the courage to make yourself.

STEM@Skook29 is Schuylkill Intermediate Unit 29's regional STEM ecosystem initiative serving Schuylkill County. “Skook” is familiar local shorthand for Schuylkill County.

Featured Programs

Chief Science Officers
