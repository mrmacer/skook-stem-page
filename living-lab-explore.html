Step 1 · Explore

Pick a tool, then go outside.

These apps and tools help students see and name what's around them. None of them are the science — they're just how students get started looking closely.

Recommended tools

Start with these four

Merlin and Seek work well for any grade band with no account required. eBird and iNaturalist add real scientific data contribution — best for teacher-managed, older-student use.

Birds · No account needed

Merlin Bird ID

Identify birds visually or by sound in real time — great for any grade band, including K–2 with teacher support.

Visit Merlin ↗
Plants, insects & more · No account needed

Seek by iNaturalist

Identify plants, insects, fungi, and other organisms with the camera — especially good for younger students since accounts aren't required.

Visit Seek ↗
Birds · Advanced / teacher-managed

eBird

Bird observation and real scientific data contribution. Best as a teacher-managed, secondary-level option once students understand the basics.

Visit eBird ↗
Biodiversity · Advanced / teacher-managed

iNaturalist

Community biodiversity observations feeding real scientific datasets — appropriate for older students under a teacher-managed account.

Visit iNaturalist ↗
