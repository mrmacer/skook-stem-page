Log a biodiversity observation

One class, one site, one observation at a time. No student names or accounts needed — just the science.

Don't include student names, emails, or other identifying information anywhere on this form — Living Lab tracks School + Site + Grade Band + Observation, never an individual student. Read our Privacy statement.

Before you submit

  • Every submission is reviewed before it appears publicly on the map or dashboard.
  • Don't see your school? Ask your teacher to contact us to get it added.
  • Coordinates are optional and only need to be approximate — general enough to identify the site, not a precise personal location.
  • Not sure what you found? Leave the species field blank and describe it in the notes instead.
