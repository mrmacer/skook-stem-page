For teachers

You don't need a new science course to participate

Start with one observation, one question, and one trip outside.

Quick start

Four participation levels

Pick a level that fits your class right now — you can move up whenever you're ready, and there's no obligation to run a massive project.

1
Explore

Take students outside and identify organisms with Merlin, Seek, or a field guide. No submission required.

2
Document

Submit periodic observations through the Living Lab form — even just once a season.

3
Investigate

Use Living Lab data (yours or the region's) to explore a real question as a class.

4
Act

Use the evidence your class gathered to design or implement an environmental solution.
