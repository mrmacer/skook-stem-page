Program 03

Chief Science Officers (CSO)

Chief Science Officers are students in grades 6–12 elected by their peers to serve as STEM ambassadors for their school and community. IU29 coordinates the active Pennsylvania chapter regionally, supporting students, advisors, and schools throughout the year.

Each CSO completes an annual leadership cycle: election, Leadership Training Institute, an initial and administrator-approved Action Plan, Fall and Spring Cabinet Meetings, Quarterly Impact Reports, and a year-end reflection. This active program is funded for three more years; it is not a pilot.

STEELS@Skook29

Pennsylvania's STEELS standards represent a major shift in how science, technology, engineering, environment, ecology, and sustainability are taught. That shift requires support — and that's what STEM@Skook29 delivers.

We provide teachers and administrators with the resources, training, and coaching they need to implement the STEELS framework with confidence — at every grade level and in every subject area.
